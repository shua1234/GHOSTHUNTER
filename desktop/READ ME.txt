Ghost Hunter web v5.1
=====================

What's new in 5.1
- A pixel-art ghost bat flaps in the bottom-left corner, blended into the app background.
- GhostHunter.exe for Windows: opens the app in its own window.
- The sound judge, video counter and everything else are the same as 5.0.

What's in this folder
- GhostHunter.exe                   Windows: double-click to open Ghost Hunter
- Ghost Hunter v5.1.html            the app (works offline, open it in any browser)
- Make desktop icon (Windows).bat   double-click to put a Ghost Hunter icon on your desktop
- ghost_hunter.ico                  desktop icon (Windows)
- ghost_hunter.icns                 desktop icon (Mac)
- ghost_hunter_icon.png             icon as a picture (phones, anything else)
- ghost_bat.gif                     the flapping bat animation

Windows
1. Unzip this folder somewhere it will stay (e.g. Documents).
2. Double-click GhostHunter.exe. It opens Ghost Hunter in its own window (using Edge or Chrome).
   The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the exe isn't signed:
   click "More info" then "Run anyway".
3. For a desktop icon, double-click "Make desktop icon (Windows).bat",
   or right-click GhostHunter.exe > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut).

Mac
1. Unzip the folder somewhere it will stay.
2. Right-click "Ghost Hunter v5.1.html" > Make Alias, and drag the alias to your desktop.
3. Open ghost_hunter_icon.png in Preview, Edit > Select All, Edit > Copy.
4. Select the alias, press Cmd+I, click the small icon at top left, then Cmd+V.

Phone
Open the online link in your browser and use "Add to Home Screen".
