🔒 Privacy & security

  • Everything runs on this device. Videos and recordings are read straight from your phone or laptop into this page's memory and are never uploaded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Internet is locked off. The page carries a security rule (Content-Security-Policy) that stops it from connecting to any server. Checking…
  • No accounts, no tracking, no ads, no cookies. It doesn't use your camera, microphone or location.
  • No back doors: there is no hidden code. Every line is in this one page (View source), and it can be checked by anyone.
  • Close the page and everything you looked at is gone.

How good is it?

  • Sound learner: the same as Ghost Hunter v5 on the PC — 80 decision trees learned from confirmed calls (TERN EcoAcoustics, expert-identified).
  • Fair test (each recording judged by a learner that never heard it): (ghost , other ).
  • 3-bat test on 32 new recordings: 31 right (97%).
  • This web version gives the same sound answers as the PC on every recording tested (24 of 24).
  • Video counting is a lighter version of the PC method so it runs on phones. Check your first videos against the PC version.
  • Species ID needs a bat detector recording (192 kHz or more). A normal trail-cam microphone stops at about 20–24 kHz: Ghost Hunter can tell a bat was heard, but won't guess the species from it.
